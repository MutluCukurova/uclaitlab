Our mission

Augmenting how people learn

Part of UCL Knowledge Lab, University College London, we study how analytics and AI can help students learn how to learn and solve complex problems together — preparing people for a future that demands far more than routine cognitive skills and traditional approaches to AI automation.

How can AI and analytics augment the way people learn?

One question drives our work across interaction, modelling, intervention, and adoption.
