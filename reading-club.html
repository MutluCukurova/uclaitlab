Reading Club & Workshop

Reading Club meetings take place once a month, either online or in the lab's small seminar room. We discuss research papers through the lens of learning analytics, AIED and data science — and run hands-on technical workshops alongside them.

Each month a member chairs the session, circulating a paper at least 10 days ahead. They open with a short briefing — no more than 15 minutes — on its strengths and weaknesses, then lead the discussion. We deliberately mix technical and theoretical work.
