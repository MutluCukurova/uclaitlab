Reading Club

Reading Club meetings take place once a month, either online or in the lab's small seminar room. We discuss research papers through the lens of learning analytics, AIED and data science.

Each month a member chairs the session, circulating a paper at least 10 days ahead. They open with a short briefing — no more than 15 minutes — on its strengths and weaknesses, then lead the discussion. We deliberately mix technical and theoretical work.

Here is the schedule — come and join us if you are interested in the title of the month, and be prepared to critique!
